Whisperer Account Coach

A strategic account coaching prototype — walk a fictional account through Perceive, Position, Perform and Progress, and get a coaching view alongside the evidence.

Live Open site

About this project

Whisperer Account Coach is a self-contained prototype for coaching account managers through a four-phase strategic review — Perceive, Position, Perform, Progress — using fictional accounts. Each phase surfaces known facts, relationship gaps and working assumptions, then pairs them with a coaching view: what deserves attention, the next honest question to ask, and a practice prompt. Account context can be edited locally to see how the coaching responds. All data is fictional and stays in the browser it's used in.
