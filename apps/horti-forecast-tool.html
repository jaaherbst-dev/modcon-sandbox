Horti Forecast Tool

A salesman-first horticulture forecasting and packaging stock-control prototype. Takes customer usage history, crop signal, and stock position and turns them into a working forecast conversation — from crop to carton.

Live Open site

About this project

Horti Forecast Tool is a working prototype for supplier reps managing customer supply for horticultural packaging. It brings together a forecast workbench, customer profile, SKU & artwork library, stock control, account activity, and weekly meeting prep into one workspace — grounded in real usage history rather than guesswork.
