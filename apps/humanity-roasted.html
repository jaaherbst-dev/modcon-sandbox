Humanity, Roasted

A sardonic, dry Australian editorial site and newsletter — six desks covering Australian affairs, geopolitics, culture, tech, Bitcoin, and people reclaiming their lives from the systems built to extract them.

Archived Open site

About this project

Humanity, Roasted was a live editorial site and weekly newsletter with a distinct sardonic voice, plus a companion coffee brand. This is a preserved snapshot of the site — pages, past editions, and design all intact — kept here for reference now that it's no longer live. Signup forms are disabled since the newsletter no longer accepts subscribers.

The AI tool that generated its weekly editions is preserved separately as its own project: Newsletter Engine.

Please send any comments or feedback to info@reclaimed-media.com.
