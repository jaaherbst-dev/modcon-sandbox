What This Is

One diagnosis.
Six desks.

The institutions are cooked. The media runs on outrage. The systems that were supposed to work demonstrably don't, and the people running them have stopped pretending otherwise.

Humanity, Roasted is a newsletter for people who see this clearly and haven't checked out. Sharp editorial across politics, geopolitics, culture, tech, money, and what it looks like to build something anyway.

Recognition, not recruitment. We don't tell you what to think. We name what's already true.

One cohesive edition. Every week. Dry enough to make you half-smile. Real enough to make you buy the coffee.
